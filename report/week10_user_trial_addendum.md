# Week 10 V10 user trial — evidence addendum

Recorded from Bao's clarification on 4 November 2026. This supplements, rather than rewrites, the pinned evidence snapshot `67ce56c`.

## Reported activity
Bao and Kerk invited several users to use VisuAlgo+ V10 independently for 15–20 minutes, recorded their interactions, administered a short post-use test and collected feedback. Participants included frequent and infrequent users of VisuAlgo.net and people with varied algorithm experience, including experienced competitive programmers and beginners.

Bao reports both positive feedback and opportunities for improvement. Reviewing video helped identify interactions supporting familiarization and areas requiring refinement. This was a separate activity from Kerk's earlier V2/V7 survey and the joint decision with Prof. Halim to adopt V10.

## Design response
Floating text means short explanations beside the relevant node or edge on the canvas. Bao identifies it as an important update after the trial, intended to minimize the need to look at Status while following visualization steps. This follows Prof. Halim's canvas-focused goal as recalled by Bao. Persistent Status text remains available. The intended reduction in attention switching has not been measured.
