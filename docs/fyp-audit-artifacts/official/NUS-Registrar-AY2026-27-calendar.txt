                                                                    MINDEF In-Camp Training

The University has a standing arrangement with the Ministry of Defence and Ministry of Home Affairs for undergraduate
NSmen from SAF, SPF and SCDF to be called up for In-Camp Training (ICT) only during specific parts of the university
vacation. There will not be any compulsory academic programme scheduled or conducted during the period reserved for
the ICTs. Undergraduate NSmen who are called up for ICT during the reserved period will not be granted deferment on
the grounds of academic commitments. The call-up periods are indicated below:
